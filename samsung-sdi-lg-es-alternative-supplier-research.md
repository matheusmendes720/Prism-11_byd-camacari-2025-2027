### HHI Diversification Effect

Adding Samsung SDI or LGES as a second supplier (each capturing ~20–30% of BYD Camaçari cell volume) would reduce CATL's share from 65% to approximately 35–45%, yielding an **estimated HHI of ~2,600–3,100** (vs. the target ~2,800).

- Both are non-Chinese (Korea-based), providing meaningful geopolitical diversification away from CATL
- Both have existing supply infrastructure in Americas (via USA plants) that could serve as import base for Brazil
- **Key risk:** Neither manufactures LFP chemistry. BYD's blade battery is LFP. NMC qualification would require BYD engineering team to validate a different chemistry platform, adding 12–24 months to qualification timeline.

## 4. Recommended Next Steps

1. **Engineering alignment first:** Confirm with BYD Camaçari procurement/tech team whether NMC pouch cells from Samsung SDI or LGES are architecturally acceptable for the target vehicle platforms (vs. CATL LFP).
2. **Supply agreement outreach:** Initiate RFQ via Samsung SDI global key accounts desk (Korea HQ) and LGES automotive sales (Korea HQ), referencing BYD Camaçari volume projections.
3. **Logistics study:** Evaluate landed cost of importing cells from USA (Samsung SDI Stellantis JV / LGES Ultium) vs. existing CATL China supply for Camaçari.
4. **Explore China-based alternatives:** Given LFP compatibility, also evaluate **EVE Energy**, **Sunwoda**, and **REPT BATTERO** — all Chinese, but potentially easier LFP qualification and already serving CATL-competitive volumes.
5. **Contact brokers:** Reach out to trade desks at **Korea Trade-Investment Promotion Agency (KOTRA)** São Paulo office — they often facilitate B2B introductions between Korean manufacturers and Brazilian buyers.
